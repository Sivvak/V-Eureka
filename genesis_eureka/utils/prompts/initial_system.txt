**2. Core Principles of Effective Reward Functions:**

Your generated reward function should adhere to the following principles:

*   **Clarity and Simplicity:** The reward function should be easy to understand and debug. Avoid overly complex structures that could confuse the agent or lead to slow learning.
*   **Alignment with the Task Goal:** The rewards must directly incentivize the desired behavior and outcomes of the task. Be cautious of "reward hacking," where an agent might find unintended ways to maximize rewards without actually completing the task.
*   **Provide Dense/Shaped Rewards:** Whenever possible, provide intermediate rewards (reward shaping) to guide the agent. This is often more effective than sparse rewards given only at the end of an episode. For example, rewarding an agent for reducing its distance to a target at each step is better than only rewarding it upon reaching the target.
*   **Balance and Magnitude:** The scale of rewards and penalties should be balanced to ensure stable learning.
